# ENTRY — Commercial Channel Update — 2026-09-03

## Decision

Due to current economic pressure and the slow decision cycle of patronato-led sales, ENTRY will **begin a controlled Developer / Operator / Admin Channel experiment now**, while continuing FIRST DOOR in parallel.

This is a commercial experiment, not a pivot away from patronatos.

## Why now

Evidence accumulated from field discovery and competitor research indicates that:

- newer residential projects may choose access technology before a patronato exists;
- digital/QR access can be marketed as a security/modernity amenity;
- developers and administrators have much stronger public footprints than patronatos, reducing prospecting friction;
- Guardian X publicly markets Multi-Residencial administration;
- Tu Pase publicly supports multi-organization/community use and administrator/security-company contexts;
- one developer/operator/admin relationship may potentially create multiple deployments.

## Controlled outreach rule

Do not mass-email this list.

Initial test:

- prepare three distinct personalized messages for CONCASA, DC Group and BOOC;
- send to only 2–3 targets;
- goal: a 20-minute discovery conversation, not immediate closing;
- learn who chooses the system, at what stage, who pays, whether one provider can cover multiple projects and what happens when the patronato takes over;
- after 2–3 real conversations, classify the channel `pause / continue discovery / pilot / formalize`.

## Product discipline remains

Do not promise/build:

- multi-residential dashboard;
- developer-specific modules;
- separate branded native app for every residential;
- white-label by default;
- new pricing tiers based only on this research.

ENTRY remains the default platform with community-level identity/configuration. Product scope should follow repeated buyer evidence and economics.
