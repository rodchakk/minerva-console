# ENTRY — Competitor Intelligence: GR Tecnologia HN

**Date:** 2026-09-06  
**Classification:** Local direct competitor / residential-software vendor / HIGH relevance  
**Status:** Market intelligence only — no product commitment

## Why this matters

Operator discovered that the app used by Residencial Toledo is published by **GR Tecnologia HN** and that the same Google Play developer account publishes multiple residential-community apps.

This is relevant because it provides another independent Honduras/San Pedro Sula market signal that residential communities are already buying and operating dedicated software for access, security and community workflows.

## Operator-supplied evidence

The operator supplied:

- Google Play developer page for **GR Tecnologia HN**;
- company website: `https://grtecnologiahn.com/`;
- a developer-page screenshot showing residential apps including:
  - Residencial Provenza;
  - Residencial Villa Valencia;
  - Terralta 02 Residencial;
  - Residencial Villas Madero;
  - Marsella Residencial;
- screenshots from three GR residential apps showing highly similar login-screen structure with community-specific branding.

The supplied login screenshots share a strong visual pattern:

- community logo / brand block at the top;
- colored header area;
- curved / wave transition into the main white content area;
- `Bienvenidos` heading;
- `Correo` and `Clave` fields;
- primary `Entrar` button;
- `Recuperar Clave` action;
- `Leer Politicas` action;
- version label near the bottom.

The major visible changes between screenshots are the **community logo, color palette and brand identity**.

### Evidence interpretation

This is strong visual evidence of a **shared reusable application shell / white-label template** that is branded and published separately for different communities.

It does **not** by itself prove:

- an identical backend for every app;
- identical feature configuration;
- identical contracts/pricing;
- that every app is produced from exactly one codebase;
- ownership/procurement relationships between the represented communities.

Treat `shared white-label platform/template` as a high-confidence working hypothesis, not a fully verified architecture statement.

## Commercial model hypothesis

GR Tecnologia HN appears to be operating a model closer to:

> **Shared residential software foundation → community-specific branding/configuration → separately published app per residential community.**

This can be commercially attractive because the buyer can perceive the result as:

> “our residential has its own app.”

That packaging may be particularly compelling for:

- new residential developments;
- developers marketing technology as an amenity;
- premium communities where branded resident experience is part of the sales proposition.

The operator specifically identified this as a potentially strong commercial idea for **new residenciales**.

## ENTRY comparison / current decision

ENTRY's current product posture remains different:

> **ENTRY is the product. Communities live inside ENTRY through configuration/identity rather than receiving a separate store app by default.**

Do **not** change that architecture or commercial promise from this discovery alone.

However, GR materially strengthens the hypothesis that **dedicated community branding / white-label packaging can carry commercial value**, particularly in the Developer / Operator / Admin channel.

White-label or dedicated-app packaging should therefore remain a possible future **enterprise/developer option**, subject to:

1. direct buyer demand;
2. contract value;
3. app-store/release/support economics;
4. maintenance complexity;
5. repeated evidence that branding materially changes close rate or developer value.

No separate ENTRY app per community is approved at this stage.

## Product-demand signals — do not copy blindly

- amenity/facility reservations;
- community communications;
- payments/account status;
- complaints/incidents;
- visitor/photo evidence;
- trash-collection notifications;
- emergency/panic workflows.

These are **market-demand signals**, not instructions to reproduce GR's feature set.

ENTRY guardrail remains:

> Validate the workflow with real ENTRY customers before promoting it into the roadmap. Do not enter feature-chasing against competitors.

## Strategic research question unlocked

The highest-value follow-up is not another feature comparison. It is understanding **how GR gets multiple residential communities**.

Research questions:

- Are several GR residential clients tied to the same developer, administrator or property group?
- Does GR sell directly to patronatos, developers or condominium administrators?
- At what stage is the software purchased: construction, presale, handoff or mature community?
- Who pays after a developer hands the community to a patronato?
- Is the dedicated branded app part of the development sales/amenity package?
- How does GR price setup, customization, publishing and ongoing support?
- Does each app receive synchronized releases or diverge by client?
- What operational burden does app-per-community create?

This directly supports the active **Developer / Operator / Admin Channel** investigation.

## Market implication

Combined with Guardian X, Tu Pase and ENTRY's own Andalucía progress, GR Tecnologia HN further supports the conclusion that:

> **The residential digital-access/community-software category already exists in Honduras. ENTRY does not need to prove the category exists; it needs to execute a differentiated product, operating model and distribution strategy.**

## Decision / guardrails

- Preserve this as competitor and channel intelligence.
- Do not attack or disparage GR in sales conversations.
- Do not copy features solely because GR ships them.
- Do not approve white-label/app-per-community architecture yet.
- Do investigate whether branded dedicated apps improve developer/new-residential sales.
- Do investigate GR's apparent multi-community acquisition/distribution path.
