## Summary

Created the first version of Minerva Core Brain's **role-based** contracts.
Contracts define responsibilities by role, not by model: a mission assigns an
available agent/model (or a human) to a role, and a model may fill different
roles in different missions. Added `content/brain/loop/contracts/` with an
index and seven contracts (orchestrator, implementer, reviewer-ci,
adversarial-auditor, senior-architect, local-triage-assistant, merge-owner),
converted `ROLES.md` from model-bound descriptions into a role-contract index,
updated the three loop templates to separate assigned role / assigned
agent-model / human merge owner, and added a short role-assignment note to
`04_WORKFLOW.md`.
